Engines: **game** = as built (8-day cap, a miss + right re-ask restores the box, ~15 engine-chosen reviews a session, quests ask random words of the zones visited); **ladder** = game + boxes 16/32/64/128 d; **steered** = game intervals but every practice moment can go to a due item; **proposed** = long ladder + honest lapses + steering.
Policies: **content-first** = the open world as built (new items whenever the content has them); **review-first** = new items only once <= 5 due items are left.
